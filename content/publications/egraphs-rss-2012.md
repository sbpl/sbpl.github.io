The E-Graph framework is the basis of multiple downstream lab
projects, including:

- [`e-graphs.git`](https://sbpl.pc.cs.cmu.edu/redmine/people/mike/e-graphs.git) — the core E-Graph algorithm
- [`fbp.git`](https://sbpl.pc.cs.cmu.edu/redmine/fbp.git) — PR2 full-body planning integration
- [`egraph-environments.git`](https://sbpl.pc.cs.cmu.edu/redmine/people/mike/egraph-environments.git) — arm + navigation domain bindings

All three repositories lived under Mike Phillips's redmine area on
the old `sbpl.pc.cs.cmu.edu` server.
