The technique introduced in this paper underlies the SBPL Lattice
Planner shipped in the [`sbpl`](https://github.com/sbpl/sbpl) library
and the ROS [`sbpl_lattice_planner`](http://wiki.ros.org/sbpl_lattice_planner)
package — still the lab's most-cited contribution for ground-vehicle
navigation.
