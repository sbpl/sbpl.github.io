A follow-up to the lab's ICRA 2010 manipulation paper. The adaptive
primitives described here are the foundation of the
[`sbpl_arm_planner`](http://wiki.ros.org/sbpl_arm_planner) ROS
package and of [PViz](https://github.com/bcohen/pviz), the PR2
visualisation utility built by Ben Cohen as part of his arm-planning
work.
